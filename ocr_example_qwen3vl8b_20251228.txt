I don't. Not anymore.

We've written the coordinates on the walls. Painted them
over the windows, scratched them into the floor. It felt
important once. We thought if we just understood the
message, we could be part of it. That if we found the
right pattern, we'd hear what no one else could.

Now the numbers are just that-numbers. No meaning. No
answer. Just an echo of something long gone, speaking to
no one.

(1974-1980)

Following establishment of consistent orbital habitats
